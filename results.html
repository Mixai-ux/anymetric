How results are verified

Metrics Agreed Before.
Measured After.

We don't measure success against deliverables. We measure it against the number we agreed to move before work began.

01 — BASELINE
Agreed metric, agreed baseline

Before any work begins, we document the current state of the target metric using the client's own analytics or agreed third-party tooling. This becomes the benchmark everything is measured against.

02 — TARGET
Written target, written guarantee

The improvement target is specified in the project agreement — not as a range, but as a floor. If we don't hit the floor within the agreed timeline, we continue at zero cost or issue a refund.

03 — MEASURE
Same method, same tools

Post-engagement results are measured with the identical methodology used to establish the baseline. We don't switch metrics or measurement tools mid-engagement. The number is the number.

FAQ

Results
Questions.

Why are your case studies anonymized?
Our clients are often enterprise brands and regulated businesses where competitive advantage depends on discretion. Many explicitly request that we not reference their company or results publicly. We respect that — the results speak for themselves without the name attached. For white-label work, NDA terms make attribution impossible by design.
How are the metric improvements verified?
Every engagement begins with an agreed baseline metric and a documented measurement methodology. Improvements are verified against the same methodology used to establish the baseline — client analytics, CRM data, or agreed third-party measurement tools. We do not present estimated or projected figures as results.
Can I speak with a previous client?
For engagements where the client has consented to serve as a reference, we can arrange an introduction. This is the exception rather than the rule given our discretion-first approach. Request this specifically in your audit form and we will check whether a reference introduction is possible for your specific industry and use case.
Do you only work with large enterprise clients?
No. The case studies shown include mid-market businesses (€5M–€50M revenue) as well as enterprise engagements. Our CEE-market pricing makes senior-calibre AI PM work accessible to businesses that would typically be priced out of equivalent Western European consultancy. The majority of our active engagements are with growth-stage businesses and founder-led companies.
