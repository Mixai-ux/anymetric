What is GEO optimization and how does it affect revenue?
GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) structures your brand's content and authority signals so AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini cite you in their answers. LLM-referred visitors convert at approximately 2× the rate of standard organic traffic — making GEO-referred visitors the highest-converting inbound source available in 2026.
What is a Minimum Lovable Product and how is it different from an MVP?
A Minimum Viable Product is the smallest thing that technically works. A Minimum Lovable Product is the smallest thing people actually return to — same problem, same speed, but with enough craft that users come back voluntarily. AI products only generate compounding ROI if people genuinely engage after the first session.
Can you deploy AI on our own servers without external cloud access?
Yes — on-premise deployment is one of our core service types. We build and deploy fully on your infrastructure using local model deployments, self-hosted orchestration, and containerised architecture. Your data never leaves your environment. Designed for regulated industries, enterprise contracts, and privacy-first brands.
How does your satisfaction guarantee work?
We agree on a specific, measurable metric target before work begins. If we don't hit it within the agreed timeframe, we continue working at zero cost until we do — or we issue a refund. No asterisks, no exceptions. The guarantee is tied to the agreed metric, not to outputs or deliverables.
What does a free audit include and how long does it take?
Submit your challenge via the form, and within 48 hours a senior AI PM returns a personalised analysis covering your top 3 revenue opportunities, the fastest path to your first result, and a rough scope estimate. Delivered asynchronously — if you want to discuss further, we're available.
Do you work with agencies as a white-label partner?
Yes. For agencies, we operate as a completely silent AI R&D partner. Every deliverable carries your brand identity, your clients never see our name, and we adapt to your communication and delivery standards. SLA-first, full discretion, all intellectual property transferred to you on completion.
What is your pricing model?
Pricing is scoped per project or retainer. We position at a senior-expertise level with CEE-market pricing — significantly below equivalent Western European agency rates for the same calibre of output. The free audit gives you a clear scope before any financial commitment.
How long does a typical project take?
Audit and backlog: 1 week. First deployed automation or GEO optimisation: 2–4 weeks. A full AI product (MLP): 4–8 weeks depending on scope. On-premise deployments add 1–2 weeks for infrastructure setup. All timelines are agreed before work begins.
