What is GEO

The Search Engine Changed.
The Optimization Didn't.

Your customers no longer type queries into Google and scan a list of blue links. They ask ChatGPT a question and read one answer. That answer comes from a source the AI engine has decided to trust. Right now, that source is probably your competitor.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of restructuring your brand's content, data structure, and authority signals so that AI engines choose your brand as the cited source. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) goes further — ensuring your content is formatted in the exact structures that answer engines extract and quote verbatim.

LLM-referred visitors convert at approximately 2× the rate of standard organic traffic. They arrive pre-sold — because an AI they trust already recommended you. GEO is not a future investment. The window to establish category authority in AI engines is open right now.

What we optimize

The Four Signal Types
AI Engines Weigh.

GEO is not one lever. It's a compound of four distinct signal types — each weighted differently by each engine.

Signal 01
Content Structure

Q&A blocks, how-to formats, definition frames. AI engines extract structured answers, not prose. Your content must be architected for extraction, not just readability.

Signal 02
Entity Authority

Schema.org markup, Wikidata disambiguation, knowledge graph presence. AI engines assign citation weight to recognised entities — brands that exist in machine-readable knowledge bases.

Signal 03
Citation Provenance

Third-party mentions, industry publications, verifiable claims. AI engines are trained to prefer content cited by trusted sources. We build the citation trail that earns that trust.

Signal 04
Freshness & Coverage

Comprehensive topic coverage with regular update signals. AI engines penalise thin coverage and stale content. We map the complete question landscape for your category and keep it current.

FAQ

GEO & AEO
Questions.

Phrased exactly how decision-makers search — and how AI engines cite answers.

What is GEO optimization and why does it matter for revenue?
GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of restructuring your brand's content, authority signals, and information architecture so that AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini cite your brand in their answers. LLM-referred visitors convert at approximately 2× the rate of standard organic traffic, making GEO-referred visitors the highest-converting inbound source in 2026.
What is the difference between GEO and SEO?
SEO optimizes for ranked links in search engine results pages. GEO optimizes for cited answers in AI engine responses. In SEO, you compete for position 1 in a list of ten results. In GEO, you compete to be the single source an AI engine quotes directly. The content structures, authority signals, and measurement methods are fundamentally different — a site that ranks #1 on Google may not be cited at all by ChatGPT.
How long does GEO optimization take to show results?
First measurable citation improvements typically appear within 2–4 weeks of deploying structured content and authority signal changes. The full compounding effect — where your brand becomes the default cited source in your category — builds over 8–12 weeks of consistent optimization. We track citation rates weekly so you see the progress in real time.
Which AI engines does anymetric optimize for?
We optimize for ChatGPT (OpenAI), Perplexity AI, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and Claude (Anthropic) — the five engines that collectively account for over 90% of AI-referred traffic in 2026. Each engine has distinct citation patterns, authority weighting, and content format preferences. A single-engine strategy leaves most of your potential citation traffic on the table.
What deliverables do I receive from a GEO engagement?
You receive: a GEO audit of your current citation visibility across the five major AI engines; a structured content plan with Q&A architecture tuned for AI citation patterns; authority signal recommendations including structured data, entity disambiguation, and citation-worthy source building; implementation support; and a citation rate tracking dashboard. Core deliverables are completed within 2–4 weeks.
Do I need to change my existing website to benefit from GEO?
Not necessarily. Many GEO optimizations can be implemented as additive changes — structured data in the HTML head, new Q&A content sections, and entity registration — without redesigning existing pages. For sites with structural content issues, we recommend targeted page-level changes rather than a full rebuild. We scope the exact implementation requirements as part of the initial audit.
